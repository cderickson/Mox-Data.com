Match-level table: Contains match data, including players, results, and deck references for each match.
Column NameDescriptionData Type
MATCH_IDUnique match identifierBIGINT
P1Player 1 Username.VARCHAR(30)
P2Player 2 Username.VARCHAR(30)
P1_WINSNumber of game wins by Player 1.INT
P2_WINSNumber of game wins by Player 2.INT
MATCH_WINNERWinner of the match (P1 or P2).VARCHAR(2)
P1_DECK_IDIdentifier referencing Player 1's deck information from VALID_DECKS.BIGINT
P2_DECK_IDIdentifier referencing Player 2's deck information from VALID_DECKS.BIGINT
P1_NOTEAdditional Notes for Player 1.VARCHAR(100)
P2_NOTEAdditional Notes for Player 2.VARCHAR(100)
EVENT_IDIdentifier referencing event information from EVENTS.BIGINT
PROC_DTTime at which record was processed.TIMESTAMP
